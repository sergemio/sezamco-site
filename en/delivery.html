Delivery FAQ

Everything you need to know before ordering

What are Sezam&Co's delivery hours?

We deliver 7 days a week, from 12pm to 11pm (last order 10:30pm). Average delivery time is 20 to 35 minutes depending on distance.

What is the minimum order for delivery?

The minimum order depends on the platform you choose (typically €10–15). Our lunch set — manakish + starter + drink — starts at €11.10.

Which Lyon districts do you deliver to?

We deliver to Lyon 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8 and Villeurbanne via Uber Eats, Deliveroo and Delicity. The exact zone depends on the platform and time of day.

Are there delivery fees?

Delivery fees are set by each platform and depend on distance. For zero fees, choose click & collect on our website.

Does the food stay hot during delivery?

Yes. Every dish is prepared to order and packed carefully to keep it hot and crispy during transport.

Is the Sezam&Co menu 100% halal?

Yes, our entire menu is 100% halal, no exceptions. Meats, sauces, breads — everything is halal.
